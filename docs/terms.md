# Nightfall Diaries — Terms of Use

**Last updated:** November 5, 2026  
**Status:** Draft for review; not a guarantee that the app is publicly available.

## Purpose
Nightfall Diaries is an owner-operated original animated-story project. The associated automation may create AI-assisted fictional videos and, once tested and authorized, send them to accounts controlled by the owner. It is not a public video-generation service; there is no subscription or user-registration service.

Stories, characters, scripts and original project-specific creative materials are intended to be original. Third-party software, model weights, music and sound effects remain governed by their respective licenses. Do not assume all repository files, generated visuals, or third-party media are free for reuse. Model and audio license compliance must be verified before commercial/monetized use.

## AI-generated media
Video, voices, images or effects may be AI-assisted. Production and posting must follow the destination platform's synthetic-content disclosure and other applicable policies. Users must not interpret fictional dramatic scenes as real people or events.

## Responsible use
Do not misuse the project to impersonate a person, scrape or re-upload protected content without permission, breach provider quotas, circumvent technical restrictions, or access accounts without authorization. App accounts and posting permissions must be granted through official providers.

## Availability
The service is experimental, depends on limited free resources, and may pause or fail. There is no promised rendering schedule, uninterrupted access, free unlimited GPU capacity, audio lip sync, high-quality visual output, or automatic public distribution. Unreviewed generated videos may not be released.

## Third-party platforms
