- For final production, each shot starts from a complete composition of character **inside** its painted forest location, *not* transparent characters pasted over low-poly silhouettes. Generate motion from the scene image, not puppet parts.
- Cut dead air against dialogue and actions; frame continuity / sound editing; no bulky dialogue boxes; no static filler; each shot must pass motion, voice, sound, background, and character QA.

## Cost reality
The official ZeroGPU demo is available to try for free, but access/quotas/queues are not guaranteed and still depend on provider conditions. The selected LTX demo uses up to a 75-second GPU reservation per invocation. 180 seconds at 5 seconds/shot would require roughly 36 individually convincing shots, plus retries and dialogue animation. The free daily allowance is insufficient for any reliable daily 3–6 minute fully automated film. No claim of zero-budget permanent capacity is made.

Alternate proof route if this fails: an official LTX video+audio demo or Wan speech-to-video public demo under strict $0 conditions, but do not silently switch to paid APIs or return to 2.5D. If no service passes a speaking-character visual proof, halt publication.
