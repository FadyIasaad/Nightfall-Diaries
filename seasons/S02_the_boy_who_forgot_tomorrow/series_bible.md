# The Boy Who Forgot Tomorrow — Season Bible

**Status:** Story, cast and voice specifications locked; reference images and audition audio must still be produced and approved.

**Logline:** A boy who remembers tomorrows uncovers a clock that erases yesterday, including a girl everyone else has forgotten.

**Theme:** Memory matters because tomorrow is uncertain.

**Finale resolution:** The city’s memory shield is disabled; erased residents return, and Noah loses future visions but regains freedom.

## Full episode arc

| EP | Title | Key story event | Cliffhanger / ending |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | A Note From Tomorrow | Noah wakes with a note in his own handwriting warning him not to enter the clock tower. | He sees himself entering it tomorrow. |
| 02 | The Girl No One Remembers | Mira claims they are friends, but every photo of her has vanished. | Her name erases itself from Noah’s notebook. |
| 03 | The Clock That Runs Backward | They discover a city clock turns backward seven minutes at midnight. | The clock strikes thirteen. |
| 04 | Seven Minutes Missing | Noah reviews missing security footage and finds an impossible extra street. | The street appears outside his window. |
| 05 | The Man in the Yellow Coat | The Clockkeeper insists forgotten days protect the city and offers Noah a deal. | He already owns a drawing of Noah’s future. |
| 06 | A Promise He Never Made | Noah finds a voice memo of a promise to Mira he cannot recall. | Mira says Noah once agreed to erase her. |
| 07 | The City Stops at Midnight | Dr. Vale reveals a memory shield resets the city whenever it detects disaster. | The shield begins resetting everyone permanently. |
| 08 | The Wrong Future | The team enters the clock system; alternate memories reveal the shield created the crisis. | The Clockkeeper locks Mira inside the memory vault. |
| 09 | One Memory to Save | Noah must sacrifice his ability to glimpse tomorrow to restore Mira and everyone else. | The lever is seconds from closing. |
| 10 | Tomorrow Begins Again | Noah disables the shield; Mira returns to everyone’s memories, the Clockkeeper changes course, and Noah learns to face uncertainty. | Finale closes the principal plot; new seasons get a different universe. |

## Continuity commands

- Characters reference `character_id` only in scenes; only `character_registry.json` determines appearance and voice.
- Do not change costume features, body proportions, accent or voice preset between episodes.
- Keep past episode chronology and unresolved clues consistent; do not invent last-minute lore that undermines the finale.
- All dialogue rendered by that character uses their locked voice and speed, unless a dramatic line uses a tiny approved pace change.
- Voice presets are provisional until auditions are heard; after approval, freeze the resulting voice profiles and audition WAV files.
- `approved_*.png` and `approved_sample.wav` are **paths to future assets**, not files supplied in this package.
