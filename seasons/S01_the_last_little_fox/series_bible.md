# The Last Little Fox — Season Bible

**Status:** Story, cast and voice specifications locked; reference images and audition audio must still be produced and approved.

**Logline:** A young fox accused of starting a forest fire follows a hidden signal to prove what really burned his home.

**Theme:** Truth and trust take courage.

**Finale resolution:** A faulty signal lantern sparked the fire; failure to repair the warning system made it worse. Ember and his former rival save stranded animals, and the forest rebuilds.

## Continuity commands

- Characters reference `character_id` only in scenes; only `character_registry.json` determines appearance and voice.
- Do not change costume features, body proportions, accent or voice preset between episodes.
- Keep past episode chronology and unresolved clues consistent; do not invent last-minute lore that undermines the finale.
- All dialogue rendered by that character uses their locked voice and speed, unless a dramatic line uses a tiny approved pace change.
- Voice presets are provisional until auditions are heard; after approval, freeze the resulting voice profiles and audition WAV files.
- `approved_*.png` and `approved_sample.wav` are **paths to future assets**, not files supplied in this package.
