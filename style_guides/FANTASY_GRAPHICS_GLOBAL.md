# V4 visual style — illustrated cinematic fantasy, zero paid image APIs

## Look

- Original painted/graphic-novel silhouettes, layered moon/forest/city, glowing magical props, colored fog, small drifting particles.
- 720×1280, 9:16, 24 FPS, 35–60 seconds. Warm/teal for animal series, purple/blue for human fantasy.
- 6–14 shots, strongest visual hook in first 2 seconds. Story turns and pauses should be scene breaks.
- Emphasis on character eyes, signature apparel/props and readable silhouette; keep characters identical within series.
- Center/mid-lower **bold white** dialogue, outlined black, 1–3 lines. Series name and episode info in footer; no fake social app UI.
- Restrained slow zoom; dramatic audio underscores synthesized from original harmonics; per-character Kokoro speech.

## Optional upgrade with your own images

Use original art or images for which you have permission. Add a shot image at `assets/scene_overrides/EPISODE_ID/SHOT_ID.png` or an approved character transparent PNG under `assets/approved_characters/`. This is never automatic fetching/copying from TikTok, Pinterest, or a film.

## Prompt tail for manual illustration tools

`original stylized cinematic fantasy graphic novel illustration, emotional and expressive, hand-painted textured background, layered mist, rich painterly highlights, carefully designed character silhouette, dramatic cinematic lighting, 9:16 vertical composition, no words, no logos, original fictional world; preserve [CHARACTER VISUAL LOCK] exactly.`

Negative: `no copied copyrighted franchise characters, no watermarks, no logos, no text, no duplicated limbs, no character costume changes, no random face changes`.

**The prompt is guidance metadata. The built-in free renderer uses its own original programmatic art. It does not secretly call a paid AI image API.**
